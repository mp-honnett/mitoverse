Neuro lab

Acetyl-CoA + choline → acetylcholine. α-Ketoglutarate ↔ glutamate → GABA. Astrocytes replenish intermediates and recycle glutamate. These branches are conceptual, not predictions of neurotransmitter levels.

Immune lab

Activated microglia can remodel metabolism. Succinate and itaconate participate in immune signalling. Dietary omega-3 and omega-6 influence lipid mediator availability. No inflammation score is calculated.

Lipid Lab

Explore dietary fatty acid availability independently of starch oxidation. Presets represent illustrative portions, not measured food composition.

Fatty acid balance

Ratio by mass of the four modelled fatty acids only. ALA is not biologically interchangeable with EPA or DHA. No optimal ratio or inflammation score is inferred.

Omega-6 signalling

Linoleic acid → arachidonic acid (regulated conversion) → membrane phospholipids → prostaglandins, leukotrienes and lipoxins. These mediators can have different inflammatory and resolving roles.

Omega-3 signalling

ALA → EPA → DHA (limited, regulated conversion); dietary EPA and DHA can also enter membrane lipid pools. EPA and DHA are precursors of several resolution-associated mediators.

Experiment: Hold starch and machinery constant, compare the presets, and observe absolute fatty acid amounts and the dietary ratio. Krebs engine output stays unchanged because lipid oxidation is not yet modelled. These inputs do not predict brain membrane composition or clinical inflammation.

Preset assumptions and scientific background

100 g bread: 0.445 g LA, 0.028 g ALA. Fish scenario adds 0.200 g LA, 0.600 g EPA and 0.900 g DHA; oil scenario adds 6 g LA and 0.010 g ALA; EPA/DHA scenario adds 0.5 g each. These are illustrative values, not product-specific analyses. Other fatty acids are omitted. Lipid presets are independent of the starch portion slider.

Background: NIH omega-3 fatty acids fact sheet.

Model assumptions

Complete hydrolysis of starch (162 g/mol per glucose residue). The fraction oxidised is the minimum of four user-selected capacity fractions. Each oxidised glucose yields 2 acetyl-CoA, 6 CO₂ and 30–32 ATP equivalents; each TCA turn yields 3 NADH, 1 FADH₂ equivalent and 1 GTP/ATP. The remaining glucose has unspecified fate. This is a stoichiometric scenario model, not a kinetic simulation or medical advice. Regeneration throughput does not measure intermediate pool sizes.
